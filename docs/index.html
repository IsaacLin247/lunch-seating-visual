Guaranteed-Friend Lunch Seating

Students confidentially list at least four friends (or none). Every two weeks the solver reseats the whole room so that everyone who submitted a list sits with exactly one familiar face and five or six new people, alternating same-grade and mixed-grade rotations. It also avoids repeating tablemates across the year, so students still meet about as many schoolmates as under random seating.

Setup: students \(i \in \{1,\dots,N\}\), tables \(t \in \{1,\dots,40\}\), capacities \(c_t \in \{6,7\}\), \(\sum_t c_t = N\); listed peers \(L_i\) with \(|L_i| \le 8\) ; \(s_i\) = listed peers at \(i\)'s table; year history \(m_{ab}\) = co-seatings of pair \((a,b)\) while neither listed the other, \(\alpha_{ab}\) = co-seatings while one listed the other.

Feasibility:

\[ \sum_{t} x_{i,t} = 1 \ \ \forall i, \qquad \sum_{i} x_{i,t} = c_t \ \ \forall t \]

Hard guarantee (restricted to same-grade listed peers on same-grade rotations):

\[ y_{i,t} \le x_{i,t}, \qquad y_{i,t} \le \sum_{j \in L_i} x_{j,t}, \qquad \sum_{t} y_{i,t} \ge 1 \]

Stage 1 pods (seed the student running out of options):

\[ i^{*} = \arg\min_{i \in U} \, |L_i \cap U| \]

Stage 2 repair (accept swap of \(i, j\) only if):

\[ s'_i \ge 1, \qquad s'_j \ge 1, \qquad \sum_{k \in A} \mathbf{1}[s'_k \ge 1] \ \ge\ \sum_{k \in A} \mathbf{1}[s_k \ge 1] \]

Stage 3 annealing (Metropolis, \(T\) geometric \(2.5 \to 0.02\), full-year history):

\[ E = \sum_{i} \Big( 1000\,\mathbf{1}[s_i \ge 1] - \max(0,\, s_i - 1) \Big) - \frac{1}{10} \sum_{(a,b)\ \text{co-seated}} \big( 5\, m_{ab} + 2\, \alpha_{ab} \big) \]
\[ P(\text{accept } \Delta < 0) = e^{\Delta / T} \]

Stage 4 exact solve (CP-SAT, seeded with Stage 3's chart as a complete hint; \(y^{(2)}_{i,t} = 1\) exactly when \(i\) sits at \(t\) with two or more listed peers; \(r_{ab} \ge x_{a,t} + x_{b,t} - 1\ \forall t\) for previous-rotation pairs):

\[ \min\ \ 300 \sum_{i,t} y^{(2)}_{i,t} + \sum_{(a,b)} w_{ab}\, r_{ab}, \qquad w_{ab} = 100 \big( 5\, m_{ab} + 2\, \alpha_{ab} \big) \]

Defenses submissions require at least 4 names or none, with at least 2 same-grade; per rotation state, a closed set \(S\) (its names stay inside) is flagged exactly when

\[ \nexists\ S = A \uplus B:\ |A|, |B| \ge 2,\ A, B\ \text{both closed} \]

where a part is closed when every submitter in it keeps a listed peer inside; a core whose followers exceed the seats of \(\lfloor |S|/2 \rfloor\) tables is flagged too.

Pipeline: pods \(\to\) repair \(\to\) anneal \(\to\) CP-SAT certify and polish.

Synthetic data at true school scale (257 students, 40 tables, 16 rotations); every chart produced by the actual solver; no real student data. Avatars are generated in the browser from anonymous ids.
