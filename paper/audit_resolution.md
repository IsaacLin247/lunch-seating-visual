The original repair state-restoration, protection of already-satisfied students during repair, empty-effective-list, capacity accounting, and random-baseline corrections remain covered by the regression suite. The finite annealing penalty is not a proof of feasibility: the non-increasing violation filter and final hard checks establish the relevant implementation invariant. The CP stage counts affected students and previous-rotation repeat pairs; its full-history guard prevents a feasible incumbent from being replaced by a worse original objective.

The cycle-extension argument is explicitly attributed to prior work, including Noga Alon's *High School Coalitions*, rather than presented as an original theorem. The related-work section distinguishes that unrestricted partition model from capacities, fixed outside submissions, and changing eligibility. References were checked against author, institution, publisher, or official solver sources.

Four additional implementation edge cases were found during this revision: stale cached summaries after an invalidated trace, injected runner results inconsistent with requested configuration, exact-public-length inference when the length exhausts the peer universe, and a feasible CP candidate being discarded because its finite-penalty cost exceeds an invalid incumbent. The CP guard now accepts a feasible candidate over an invalid incumbent regardless of soft cost; feasible incumbents retain the original cost guard. Focused regressions and implementation hardening address these after the executable study snapshot is frozen. All517 retained rotation records were checked: the seven invalid annealing endpoints either had no initial CP candidate or already accepted a lower-cost feasible candidate, so the new priority guard changes no recorded decision. The actual standard runner and cap8/N257 study do not invoke the affected mismatched-configuration or universe-saturation cases. The assembler independently rejects stale or mismatched evidence in all cases.

Remaining limits are stated in the article: no complete strategy-resistance or candidate-completeness result; no global feasibility consequence of minimum-name rules; no exhaustive adaptive attacker search; no real network calibration, pilot, causal wellbeing evidence or comprehensive fairness evaluation; no optimal full-year certificate; no privacy-preserving mechanism. These are scope boundaries and future research questions, not claims silently treated as resolved. The missing original solver and period/collusion result files remain unverifiable.
